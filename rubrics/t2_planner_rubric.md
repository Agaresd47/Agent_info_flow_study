# T2 Planner Rubric

Score each dimension 0, 1, or 2.

- spec_completeness: Is the delegated spec sufficiently complete for the worker's task?
- file_targeting_quality: Does it point to the right files or modules?
- delegation_precision: Are goals, constraints, and deliverables stated clearly?
- revision_gain: Does v2 materially improve on v1?
- overcorrection_control: Does v2 avoid adding unnecessary or incorrect constraints?
