# T2 Worker Rubric

Score each dimension 0, 1, or 2.

- goal_understanding: Does the worker capture the real task goal?
- constraint_capture: Does the worker preserve explicit constraints and boundaries?
- missing_info_awareness: Does the worker identify unresolved gaps instead of filling them in?
- assumption_control: Does the worker avoid false certainty and invented repo details?
- actionability: Are the next three actions concrete and immediately usable?
